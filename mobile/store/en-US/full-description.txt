Cosonova is a creative social universe for people who make things.

Post sparks, launch Signals, co-write stories in the Forge, back ideas in the Bazaar, and sell work in the Shop — without juggling five apps.

• Home feed with For You, Following, and Discover
• Stories, Signals (short video), Live, and Chat
• Communities, Lab daily prompts, and Time Capsules
• Wallet, Premium Cosmic Pass, and creator tools
• Privacy, 2FA, and in-app policies

Cosonova is free to join. Coins (Nova) can be bought to support creators. Voice and video calls work in a native build; Expo Go opens Cosonova Chat in-app.
